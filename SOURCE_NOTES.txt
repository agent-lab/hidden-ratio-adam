Packaging edits:
- The paper codebook is the internal default; examples no longer repeat its values.
  Explicit custom tables and y_table=None legacy modes remain supported.
- Relative default paths for CIFAR; model definitions and training loops preserved.
- Added explicit adam_nsr_eps0 selection and one zero-epsilon pretraining YAML.
- Corrected accidental Python==3.12.0 patch pin to >=3.12,<3.13 and included
  pretraining support modules / data.datasets in packaging metadata.
- ReMax example actor defaults to the verified Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct identity.
- Post-training examples default to console logging, without shell command tracing.
- Updated launch options to the bundled verl schema: reward.* namespace,
  removal of unsupported use_importance_sampling / use_reward_loop options,
  and explicit addition of the supported SFT chat-template-file option.
- Retained upstream runtime, licenses and notices; removed development tooling,
  unrelated examples, docs/tests and agent/editor metadata.
- No large-beta update-first performance variant, raw logs, data or model weights.

Scope: compact source release, not a frozen environment or per-run result archive.
